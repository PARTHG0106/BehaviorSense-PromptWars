# RGB pilot outcome — 13 October 2026

**The optimized pipeline passed throughput, but the trained RGB pilot failed the
predeclared quality gate.** The existing [Kaggle version 1](https://www.kaggle.com/code/krishnagupta02468/bs-rgb-prefetch-20261013)
completed in **33 minutes 37 seconds**, within its 75-minute pilot allowance.
All 113 pilot videos were prepared and both heads completed training. Full-data
training and test evaluation were stopped by the gate. No new job was submitted
while recovering and auditing this result.

## Verified validation result

Both heads use the same **31,859 fitting windows from 36 videos across nine
subjects** and the complete **63,220 validation windows from 77 videos across
p04/p15**. RGB adds frozen scene/person Video Swin-T embeddings to the compact
pose/object control. Metrics below use the saved validation thresholds.

| Metric | Matched control | RGB pilot | Required RGB result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| mAP | 40.69% | **48.11%** | ≥63.79% |
| mAP gain over control | — | **+7.42 percentage points** | ≥3 points |
| Macro F1 | 43.55% | 48.82% | — |
| Micro F1 | 51.95% | 62.41% | — |
| TV/drinking pair precision | 9.43% | **36.11%** | ≥30% |
| TV/drinking pair recall | 31.22% | **6.34%** | ≥30% |
| TV/drinking pair F1 | 14.48% | **10.79%** | ≥30% |
| TV/drinking pair AP | 8.00% | 19.85% | — |

RGB beats its matched pilot control but remains below the stronger saved
ensemble's **61.79% validation mAP**. That ensemble was fitted on the full fitting
set; comparison with this subset pilot does not measure the effect of RGB at equal
full-data coverage. Its **65.29% test mAP** remains the earlier exploratory test
reference. There is no new RGB test result or serving-model replacement.

Six of nine gate checks failed: overall mAP, pair F1, pair recall, p04 mAP,
p04 pair F1, and p15 mAP. Subject mAP was **45.30% for p04** against 59.13%, and
**54.20% for p15** against 64.50%. The gate was not changed after these scores.

## What the failure shows

RGB contains useful ranking information: AP increased most for using-device
(+44.73 points), drinking (+21.99), and reading (+19.74). TV AP decreased from
48.19% to 46.32%. The improvement is therefore uneven across activities.

The gate's pair labels require both activities to be positive in the retained
window. Of **205** such validation windows, RGB predicted both for **13**,
drinking alone for **50**, TV alone for **73**, and neither for **69**. It made
36 pair predictions in total. Each marginal threshold was chosen for its own F1
(drinking 0.92864; TV 0.53307); good marginal thresholds do not guarantee good
recall for their conjunction. Pair AP improved, but the saved operating point
missed most pairs. The separate annotation-overlap diagnostic also remains weak:
14 of 272 windows with actual temporal overlap were detected (**5.15% recall**).

The pilot covers only **14.7% of fitting windows** and 36 of 274 fitting videos.
All 15 supervised activities are present, but that does not establish diversity:
cleaning positives occur in one video from one subject; hygiene has 15 positive
windows across two subjects; eating has 84 across three subjects. These are
limitations of this pilot, not proof that full-data RGB training would succeed.

RGB peaked at epoch 5 and stopped after epoch 17; the control peaked at epoch 7
and stopped after epoch 19. Both completed the prescribed 12-epoch plateau.
RGB training loss continued falling after its validation peak. The result does
not support simply adding epochs. It also does not establish the ceiling of a
fine-tuned video model: this experiment froze the Kinetics-400 encoder and trained
a small MLP on pooled features.

## Throughput is now sufficient for this pilot

The earlier native-padding comparison achieved a measured **2.30×** speedup.
This job compared native sequential extraction with native extraction overlapping
CPU preparation and a single inference worker. The paired result was a further
**1.64× aggregate speedup** on the same three complete fitting videos:

| Video | Sequential | With overlap | Speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P03T03C04 | 25.99 s | 15.08 s | 1.72× |
| P25T06C05 | 26.69 s | 17.77 s | 1.50× |
| P07T06C05 | 10.79 s | 5.91 s | 1.83× |

All **1,915 paired windows** have bit-identical embeddings and identical
row/clock/crop arrays in the six downloaded feature files. The probe finished in
126.15 seconds. Its conservative forecast was **55.71 minutes for the pilot** and
**4.57 hours for a full job**. The full-job value is a forecast, not a measured
full training duration. The measured pilot, including preparation and training,
finished in 33.61 minutes.

## Audit and follow-up

The local audits verified the frozen source archive and submission, GPU/runtime
identity, extraction-receipt coverage, checkpoint metadata and normalization,
training histories, and checkpoint/threshold/gate hashes. Every saved validation
row, label and mask matches the original benchmark. Original annotations were
replayed and all aggregate/per-subject metrics and gate decisions reproduced
without changing thresholds.

These checks do not independently rerun video encoding or checkpoint inference.
Only the six comparison feature files were downloaded for a byte-level audit;
the remaining pilot feature coverage was checked through frozen extraction
receipts and manifests. The benchmark has already been inspected and remains
exploratory, with only two validation subjects.

The review also found a metadata error: both training histories contain two
learning-rate reductions, while their convergence records say zero. It does not
affect the chosen epochs or quality decision. Future runs now record actual
learning-rate reductions. A separate edge case is also fixed: epoch-limit
exhaustion retains the truthful `incomplete_epoch_limit` status and its pilot/full
scope through the supervisor. Neither fix changes this frozen result.

The relevant training, runtime and launcher suites passed **80 tests**, with one
Linux-specific process-cleanup test skipped on Windows; 14 parameterized subtests
also passed. Four new regressions cover the reduction counters and the pilot,
full-training and supervisor paths for epoch exhaustion. Historical checkpoint
audits bind to the immutable source archive and remain reproducible after these
future-run source changes.

The next useful experiment should first use cached features and fitting-only
grouped splits to compare regularization and a pair-aware objective/calibration
rule. Any new extraction pilot should require positive examples across multiple
videos and subjects. Threshold changes alone cannot repair the **15.68-point mAP
gap** to the absolute gate. No further GPU run or automatic continuation was
started during this audit.
