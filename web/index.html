Try the care workflow in two minutes
  1. Choose “Try a fictional care profile” below. It creates a private example with a dose due now and an appointment.
  2. Record a taken or skipped confirmation, with an optional note. Check today's counts and the history.
  3. Add or edit a prescribed medication, then update the doctor appointment.
  4. Review email reminder previews, switch languages, and try the large-text or high-contrast controls.
  5. Print the care plan for a visit. Open Activity observations to explore the optional evidence-checked video reports.
